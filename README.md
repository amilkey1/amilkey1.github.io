## About Me
I am a PhD student in [Dr. Paul Lewis' lab](https://plewis.github.io/) in the [UConn EEB department](https://eeb.uconn.edu/). My research focuses on statistical phylogenetics. Currently, I'm working on projects developing 1) a novel Bayesian approach to sampling species trees under the multispecies coalescent and 2) a novel method to estimate Bayesian phylogenetic information content.
